Scenefy Privacy Policy

Effective July 29, 2026 · Applies to Scenefy for Mac and iPad

In short: Scenefy has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. Your topics, scripts, recordings, and projects are processed and stored on your own device. We do not receive them, and we could not read them if we wanted to.

Our philosophy

Privacy isn't a feature. It's the foundation. Scenefy is built on a single conviction: your creative work belongs to you — not a server, not a company, not a cloud. Every word you write, every voice you record, every video you export stays exactly where it should.

Most AI tools send your content to remote servers to process it — your scripts, your voice, your ideas travel across the internet and live on someone else's infrastructure. We think that's the wrong model. Scenefy is built around Apple Intelligence and Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework. Script generation, voice synthesis, quiz creation, and scene suggestions all run through Apple Intelligence on your local hardware, using no external AI service, no API key, and no account.

Scenefy is a sandboxed Mac App Store and iPad App Store app. Apple's sandbox prevents any app from accessing files or network resources it wasn't explicitly granted, so your content is structurally isolated to your device unless you choose to send it somewhere.

We collect no analytics, no usage data, and no crash reports that include your content. We don't know what you're making, and we don't want to. There are no background sync processes, no deferred uploads, and no "improve our AI" opt-outs buried in settings — the app does exactly what it says.

A small number of features you choose to use — importing a web page by URL, attaching a link in Assistant chat, or importing a remote file — need network access to fetch that page or file, and Scan Document and Assistant chat can read text from images, PDFs, and pages you provide. Those requests go only to the address you gave, never to Scenefy's own servers, and every other feature, including all AI generation and every export (MP4, PDF, HTML quizzes, SRT captions, and audio), works completely offline.

At a glance

Scenefy is designed to keep your creative work private. Prompts, generated content, and narration are processed on your device. Saved projects and generated media remain on your device unless you choose an action that sends or saves them elsewhere.

Apple Intelligence

Scenefy uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models for generative features. Scenefy does not send your prompts or generated responses to an external AI service, does not require an AI account or API key, and does not use that content to train its own models.

Apple Intelligence is provided by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy protections and applicable terms. You can review Apple Intelligence activity in Settings or System Settings under Privacy & Security → Apple Intelligence Report. Read Apple Intelligence & Privacy.

Information Scenefy handles

Scenefy handles the topics, instructions, scripts, projects, images, video, audio, and other files you choose to create or import. This information is used to provide the feature you requested.

Microphone, camera, photo-library, speech-recognition, Personal Voice, and file access are requested only when a feature needs them. You control these permissions in system Settings.

Storage, sharing, and deletion

Projects and generated media remain in local app storage or a location you select. Scenefy does not include advertising analytics or sell your personal information.

Content leaves Scenefy only when you deliberately export, share, open a remote source, or save to a location or service you selected. Those destinations apply their own privacy policies.

You can delete saved projects and generated media from Scenefy. Files you exported, shared, or copied elsewhere must be deleted from those destinations separately. Removing the app may remove locally stored app data, subject to device backups and system behavior.

Purchases

Scenefy Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Payment, billing, and subscription records are handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Scenefy never sees your payment details. The app stores only whether a valid subscription exists, and it stores that on your device.

Your choices

Do not enter confidential, sensitive, or regulated information unless you are permitted to use it and accept the device-level processing involved. You can disable app permissions or Apple Intelligence in system Settings, though related features may stop working.

Inner Compass

Inner Compass may process the situation, mood, and support preferences you choose to enter using Apple Intelligence on your device. Avoid names, identifying details, health records, and other highly sensitive information. Generated sessions and synthesized narration remain local unless you save, export, share, or open them in another destination.

Inner Compass applies an on-device safety check before generation. It is not a clinical assessment, diagnosis, monitoring service, or guarantee that urgent language will always be detected.

Children's privacy

Scenefy is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your region, if higher). Scenefy does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because on-device processing never leaves the app without your action, no data is transmitted to us in the ordinary course of use; if you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will help you address it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act may give you rights to access, correct, delete, or export your information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because Scenefy processes and stores your content on your own device rather than on our servers, you can exercise most of these rights directly:

  • Access and export — open any project in the app, or export it as a file.
  • Correct — edit any project, scene, or script directly.
  • Delete — remove projects from the Library, or delete the app to remove its local data.
  • Restrict processing — turn off Apple Intelligence or any app permission in system Settings.

We hold no copy of your content, so there is no account for us to delete on your behalf. For anything Scenefy cannot do locally, contact us using the details below.
