Getting started

How do I make my first video?

Open Scenefy and pick a format from the home screen — for example Explainer or Quiz. Enter a topic, then tap the button at the bottom of the form. Scenefy writes the script, builds the scenes, and narrates them. When the preview looks right, choose Export to render an MP4.

Where do my exported files go?

Wherever you choose. Scenefy is sandboxed, which means it can only read and write the exact files and folders you select in the save or open panel. When an export produces several files at once — the four-format remix, the bulk queue, or the YouTube kit — Scenefy asks you to pick a destination folder instead of a single file. That is a macOS requirement, not Scenefy browsing your disk.

Apple Intelligence

Scenefy says generation is unavailable. What now?

All AI generation runs on your device. It needs three things to be true:

  • A device that supports Apple Intelligence.
  • Apple Intelligence turned on in System Settings (Mac) or Settings (iPad).
  • The on-device models finished downloading. This happens in the background after you enable Apple Intelligence and can take a while on a slow connection.

Scenefy shows the current status in Settings → Apple Intelligence. Everything that is not generation — editing, branding, preview, and export — keeps working even when generation is unavailable.

Is my content sent anywhere?

No. Your topics, scripts, and projects are processed on your device and are never uploaded to us or to any third party. Scenefy has no accounts and no sign-in. The only time the app uses the network is when you paste a link into URL Import, and when it talks to the App Store about a purchase.

Subscriptions and purchases

What do I get for free?

Scenefy starts with 15 days of unrestricted access — unlimited AI generation, no waiting, and clean exports at full resolution. After that the free plan continues to work: a limited number of AI generations, then a short wait between them, and unlimited exports at 720p with a Scenefy watermark.

What does Scenefy Pro add?

Unlimited AI creation, exports up to 4K, and no watermark.

I paid already — how do I get it back on a new Mac?

Open Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Your plan and its expiry date are shown in the same section.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On a Mac open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings → Manage next to Subscriptions. On iPad open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro keeps working until the date shown in Scenefy.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. We are not able to issue refunds ourselves, but email us if something went wrong and we will help you sort it out.

Common problems

The microphone does not record.

Scenefy needs microphone permission before it can record narration. On a Mac check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone; on iPad check Settings → Scenefy. If you also want spoken words turned into on-screen text, Speech Recognition must be allowed as well.

Export is slower than I expected.

Some scenes have to be drawn exactly rather than through the fast path — a logo watermark is the usual reason. Longer projects and 4K output also take proportionally more time. Rendering continues while you work in another app.

My generated content is not quite right.

AI output is a starting point, not a finished script. Every scene is editable, and regenerating with a more specific topic and audience usually helps. Always review generated text before you publish it.

Privacy

Scenefy collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising identifiers. The full policy is in the app under Settings → Privacy & Legal → Privacy Policy.
